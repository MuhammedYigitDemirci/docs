# Clannect Refund Policy

Last Updated: 3/2/2026

This Refund Policy explains the current status of payments, purchases, and potential refunds on Clannect.

At this time, Clannect does not sell any products, subscriptions, digital assets, or in app currency. Therefore, no paid transactions are currently processed through the platform.

## 1. Current Status

As of the date of this policy:

- Clannect does not offer paid memberships.
- Clannect does not sell digital currency.
- Clannect does not process user payments.

Since there are no active sales, there are no refund procedures currently in effect.

## 2. Upcoming Premium Features

Clannect plans to introduce paid features in the near future, including:

- Premium subscription plans  
- A platform based digital currency known as Cloin  

Once these features become available, this Refund Policy will be updated to clearly define:

- Pricing structures  
- Billing terms  
- Subscription renewal terms  
- Refund eligibility conditions  
- Cancellation procedures  

Users will be notified of material changes before paid services become active.

## 3. Future Refund Conditions

When premium subscriptions and Cloin purchases are introduced, refund eligibility may depend on factors such as:

- Whether the purchase was used or consumed  
- The timing of the refund request  
- Applicable consumer protection laws  
- Payment provider policies  

Detailed refund rules will be published at the time paid services launch.

## 4. Third Party Payment Providers

Future payments may be processed through third party payment providers. Any transactions handled by such providers may also be subject to their own terms and refund policies.

Clannect will not be responsible for policies imposed by external payment processors, app stores, or financial intermediaries.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Refund Policy at any time.

When Clannect begins offering paid services, this document will be revised accordingly. Continued use of the platform after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Refund Policy.

## 6. Contact

If you have questions regarding payments or future premium features, you may contact us at:

support@clannect.com

By using Clannect, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Refund Policy.
