# Clannect Refund Policy

**Last Updated: 8/21/2026**

This Refund Policy explains the terms and conditions applicable to purchases made through Clannect, including **Clannect Premium subscriptions** and **Cubes**, Clannect's in-app currency.

By completing a purchase on Clannect, you acknowledge and agree to the terms described in this policy.

## 1. Clannect Premium Subscriptions

Clannect Premium is a paid monthly subscription currently priced at **\$4.99 USD per month**.

### 1.1 Refunds

Clannect Premium purchases are generally **non-refundable** once the subscription has been activated and Premium benefits have been made available to the user.

Because Premium benefits may become available immediately after purchase, users should review the subscription benefits and pricing carefully before completing a purchase.

Where required by applicable consumer protection laws, mandatory statutory refund rights will continue to apply.

### 1.2 Cancellation

Users may cancel their Clannect Premium subscription at any time.

Cancellation prevents future subscription renewals but does not automatically result in a refund for the current billing period.

After cancellation, Premium benefits remain available until the end of the current paid billing period unless otherwise required by applicable law.

## 2. Cubes Purchases

**Cubes** are Clannect's virtual, in-app currency.

Cubes are available through several purchase bundles and can be used for eligible digital items, cosmetics, customization options, and other in-app features.

Cubes purchases are generally **non-refundable once the Cubes have been credited to the user's account**.

This is because Cubes are digital content that may become available for immediate use after purchase.

If Cubes have already been spent, transferred, or otherwise used within Clannect, the purchase will generally not be eligible for a refund, except where required by applicable law.

## 3. Incorrect or Unauthorized Purchases

If you believe a purchase was made without your authorization, or if you believe you were charged incorrectly, please contact Clannect Support as soon as possible.

When contacting us, please provide relevant information such as:

- Your Clannect username or account information
- The date of the transaction
- The purchase or transaction reference, if available
- A description of the issue

We may investigate the transaction and determine whether a refund, correction, or other resolution is appropriate.

## 4. Technical Issues

If you paid for a product or digital service but did not receive the purchased content due to a technical issue, please contact Clannect Support.

For example, this may include:

- Cubes not being credited after a successful payment
- Premium not being activated after a successful payment
- A purchased digital item not being delivered correctly
- A payment being completed but the associated product not becoming available

We will investigate legitimate delivery or technical issues and, where appropriate, provide the purchased content, correct the issue, or issue an appropriate refund.

## 5. Payment Providers

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Transactions may also be subject to the terms and policies of the applicable payment provider.

Where a payment provider has specific procedures for handling unauthorized transactions, payment disputes, or refunds, users may also be required to follow those procedures.

Clannect does not control the independent policies or processing procedures of third-party payment providers.

## 6. Chargebacks

We encourage users to contact Clannect Support before initiating a payment dispute or chargeback for an issue that can be resolved directly.

Unauthorized transactions, duplicate charges, or technical payment issues should be reported to us so that we can investigate the situation.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection or payment laws.

## 7. Consumer Protection Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to remove, restrict, or override any mandatory rights granted to consumers under applicable laws and regulations.

Where applicable law provides a right to a refund, cancellation, withdrawal, or other consumer remedy, those rights will continue to apply regardless of the general terms described in this policy.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

Clannect reserves the right to update or modify this Refund Policy from time to time.

When material changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised **Last Updated** date.

Your continued use of Clannect after the effective date of an updated policy constitutes acceptance of the revised policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.
