# Clannect Posting Rules

Last Updated: 8/25/2026

These Posting Rules apply to everything you publish on Clannect: posts, clips, images, videos, comments, messages, profiles, community names, and any other content you upload or share.

They are part of the [Terms of Service](/policies/terms-of-service). If the two documents ever conflict, the Terms of Service takes priority.

---

## How these rules work

**We moderate behavior, not opinions.** You can disagree, criticize, argue, joke, and hold unpopular views on Clannect. What we act on is conduct: harassment, threats, fraud, deception, privacy violations, safety risks, and platform abuse.

**Context matters.** Before we act, we look at what was posted, who it was aimed at, how severe it was, whether it was presented as fact or opinion, whether real harm was likely, and what your history looks like.

**You do not need bad intent to break a rule.** Some rules exist because the risk is foreseeable. If you post a rapidly flashing clip with no warning, you may not have meant to hurt anyone, and it can still be a serious violation. Intent matters most when we decide how far to escalate.

**Enforcement usually escalates.** Most rules follow a ladder: removal, warning, short suspension, longer suspension, permanent suspension. Severe violations skip the ladder.

**We explain our decisions.** When we remove content or restrict your account, we tell you which rule applied and why.

## 1. Harassment and threats

You may not target another person with abuse, threats, or sustained unwanted attention.

Not allowed:

- Threats of violence, harm, or death against anyone
- Targeted insults, degradation, or humiliation aimed at a specific person
- Repeated unwanted contact after someone has asked you to stop
- Organizing or encouraging others to pile onto a person or community
- Coordinated harassment campaigns, including brigading from outside Clannect
- Threatening to expose someone's private information

Allowed:

- "This developer made a terrible design decision and the patch made the game worse."
- Harsh reviews, negative feedback, and public criticism of companies, creators, and public figures
- Arguing with someone who is arguing back

Not allowed:

- "Everyone go spam this developer until they quit."
- Following someone across the platform to attack them in every thread

**Enforcement**

- First offense: content removal and a warning
- Repeat offense: 7-day, then 30-day suspension
- Severe offense: immediate permanent suspension for credible threats of violence, coordinated harassment campaigns, or harassment that continues after a suspension

## 2. Child safety

This is an absolute rule with no exceptions and no warnings.

Not allowed:

- Any sexual content involving minors, real or generated, including drawings, edits, and AI output
- Sexualizing a minor in any way, including captions, edits, and commentary
- Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to arrange contact with a minor
- Sharing or requesting links to child sexual abuse material
- Threatening to distribute intimate images of a minor

**Enforcement**

Immediate permanent suspension. We preserve evidence and report to the relevant authorities and reporting bodies where the law requires or permits it.

If you encounter this content on Clannect, report it and do not share it further, including to warn others.

## 3. Violence, gore, and disturbing content

Not allowed:

- Real-world graphic gore, mutilation, or severe injury
- Real-world death footage
- Content whose main purpose is to shock people with graphic real-world violence
- Glorifying or celebrating a real violent attack or its perpetrator

Allowed with a **Disturbing Content** label:

- Video game violence and gore
- Fictional horror, special effects, and obviously fake blood
- Intense but unrealistic fictional injuries

Allowed:

- News reporting, documentary footage, and educational or historical material about real violence, when it is presented in that context and carries a **Disturbing Content** label. Gratuitous close-ups are still removed even in a news context.

**Enforcement**

- Missing label on fictional content: label applied and, for repeat cases, content removal and a warning
- Real-world gore: content removal and a warning, then escalating suspensions
- Severe offense: content posted to shock, mock victims, or target a specific person may result in immediate permanent suspension

## 4. Flashing lights and seizure risk

Flashing content can cause seizures. Treat this as a safety rule, not a formatting rule.

You must give a clear warning **before** hazardous flashing content starts. This includes rapid flashing, strobing, rapidly alternating colors, fast image cycling, and high-frequency flash edits.

A warning that appears at the same time as the flashing, or after it has started, does not count.

Your warning must be immediately understandable and easy to see. Apply the **Flashing Lights** label, and where the effect is intense, include an on-screen warning such as:

```
⚠️ FLASHING LIGHTS WARNING
This video contains rapidly flashing lights and images that may
trigger seizures or other adverse reactions. Viewer discretion is advised.
```

A warning does not count if it is buried in a long description, hidden in hashtags, placed only in the comments, shown in tiny text, or displayed on top of the flashing itself.

**What this rule is not about.** Ordinary video transitions, muzzle flashes, explosions, gameplay effects, and occasional brightness changes are not hazardous flashing. We look at frequency, intensity, duration, contrast, and pattern, and whether the effect is reasonably likely to present a seizure risk. You are not expected to diagnose anyone or know exactly who might be affected. You are expected to warn people when your content clearly carries that risk.

**Enforcement**

- First offense: content removal or a label applied, plus a warning explaining why the content needed one. A 3-day suspension applies where the hazard was obvious and the content reached a large audience.
- Second offense: content removal and a 30-day suspension
- Third offense: content removal and permanent suspension
- Severe offense: extremely intense or prolonged flashing, hiding flashing effects to evade moderation or trick viewers, or continuing after being told about the risk may be escalated at any step

**Deliberately harmful flashing content**

Content made or sent to trigger a seizure, cause physical harm, or exploit someone's medical condition is a severe safety violation. This includes targeting a user known to have photosensitive epilepsy, hiding hazardous effects in content designed to trick someone into viewing it, and encouraging others to do the same.

This results in immediate permanent suspension regardless of your history. Where appropriate and legally permitted, we preserve evidence and refer the matter to the authorities.

## 5. Self-harm and suicide

Not allowed:

- Encouraging, glorifying, or promoting suicide, self-harm, or disordered eating
- Instructions, methods, or techniques for self-harm or suicide
- Challenges, pacts, or games that push people toward self-harm
- Telling someone to hurt or kill themselves, including as a joke or an insult

Allowed:

- Talking about your own experience, recovery, or mental health
- Support, awareness, and prevention content
- Fiction and art that deals with these themes, with a **Mature Themes** label

**Enforcement**

- Encouraging or instructing: content removal and a 30-day suspension, or permanent suspension where a specific person was targeted
- Telling a specific person to kill themselves: content removal and a 30-day suspension, and permanent suspension if it is repeated or part of targeted harassment under Rule 1
- We may show support resources alongside removals and may contact people who appear to be at risk

## 6. Sexual content

Clannect is open to users aged 13 and over, so explicit sexual content is not permitted anywhere on the platform.

Not allowed:

- Pornography and explicit sexual imagery or video
- Explicit written sexual content
- Sexual solicitation, including advertising or linking to sexual services or paid explicit content
- Non-consensual intimate imagery, including "revenge porn" and AI-generated nudes of a real person
- Sexualized content built around a real person who has not consented to it

Allowed:

- Swimwear, gym, cosplay, and beach content in ordinary contexts
- Art, fiction, and clips with romantic or suggestive themes, labeled **Mature Themes** where appropriate
- Sex education, health information, and discussion of sexuality
- Nudity in a clear artistic, medical, educational, or news context, labeled appropriately

**Enforcement**

- First offense: content removal and a warning
- Repeat offense: 7-day, then 30-day suspension
- Severe offense: non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual solicitation, and commercial pornography accounts result in immediate permanent suspension

## 7. Illegal and dangerous activity

You may not use Clannect to facilitate, promote, or coordinate serious criminal activity.

Not allowed:

- Terrorism, violent extremism, and content promoting or recruiting for organizations that carry out violence
- Selling or sourcing drugs, weapons, stolen goods, stolen accounts, or stolen data
- Coordinating serious violent crime
- Human trafficking and criminal exploitation

Allowed:

- Discussion, debate, news, education, history, and research about any of these subjects
- Documentaries, journalism, and academic analysis
- Personal accounts and harm-reduction information
- Fiction and gameplay depicting crime

The line is between talking about something and helping it happen.

**Enforcement**

- Facilitation or promotion: content removal and a suspension, scaled to severity
- Severe offense: terrorism, trafficking, exploitation, and criminal sales result in immediate permanent suspension and, where legally required, referral to the authorities

## 8. Dangerous instructions

Not allowed:

- Working instructions for making weapons, explosives, or other devices designed to injure people
- Step-by-step guidance for carrying out serious violent or criminal acts
- Instructions for defeating safety systems where the result is serious physical danger

Allowed:

- Explaining how something works at a conceptual level
- Chemistry, engineering, firearms, and history discussion and education
- Security research, capture-the-flag write-ups, vulnerability disclosure, and defensive tooling
- Fiction and gameplay

**Enforcement**

- First offense: content removal and a warning
- Repeat offense: escalating suspensions
- Severe offense: content that meaningfully enables mass harm results in immediate permanent suspension

## 9. Misinformation and deception

We are deliberately narrow here. Being wrong on the internet is not a violation, and moderators do not settle political arguments.

**Opinions, predictions, interpretation, and argument are always allowed**, including strongly worded ones about politics, policy, companies, and public figures. So are jokes, satire, and questions.

We act on false claims in these cases:

- **Fabricated or manipulated media** presented as real, including deepfakes, doctored screenshots, and edited footage passed off as genuine
- **Deception for gain**, where the false claim supports a scam, a fake giveaway, impersonation, or engagement farming
- **Safety-critical falsehoods** likely to cause real-world harm, such as false emergency or crisis information, or dangerous medical claims presented as fact
- **Claims about Clannect itself** that are false and damaging, such as fake staff announcements or fake moderation notices

Allowed:

- "I strongly oppose this policy because I believe it would harm LGBTQ people."
- "I think these proposals would damage women's rights."
- "I heard the studio is shutting down, can anyone confirm?"
- Being sincerely mistaken about a fact and correcting or leaving it up

Not allowed:

- A doctored screenshot of a Clannect staff message, posted as real
- "This charity wallet is verified by Clannect" when it is not
- A fake evacuation notice during an ongoing emergency
- An AI-generated video of a real person saying something they never said, posted as genuine

**Enforcement**

- First offense: content removal, a warning, and an explanation of what was inaccurate
- Second offense: 7-day suspension after you have already been corrected and continue posting the same claim as established fact
- Third offense: 30-day suspension
- Fourth offense: permanent suspension, treated as deliberate deception
- Severe offense: falsehoods spread to cause panic, enable fraud, or incite harm result in immediate permanent suspension

You are not required to agree with our assessment. Repeating a corrected claim as established fact after being given credible sources is what escalates, not disagreement itself.

## 10. Scams and fraud

Not allowed:

- Fake or fraudulent giveaways
- Phishing, credential harvesting, and fake login pages
- Fake payment requests, fake sales, and non-delivery of promised goods
- Investment, crypto, and "double your money" scams
- Deceptive account trading and account recovery scams
- Any attempt to obtain money, accounts, items, or personal information through deception

Allowed:

- Genuine giveaways with clear, honest terms
- Legitimate sales, commissions, and services allowed by the [Terms of Service](/policies/terms-of-service)

**Enforcement**

Scams are treated as severe. A first offense that clearly attempts to defraud users results in permanent suspension. Ambiguous cases, such as a poorly run but genuine giveaway, get removal and a warning.

## 11. Malicious links and files

Not allowed:

- Malware, ransomware, trojans, credential stealers, and account theft tools
- Phishing pages and links designed to compromise a device or account
- Deliberately harmful files, including files disguised as something else
- Links that hide their real destination in order to trick people

Allowed:

- Security education, write-ups, and analysis
- Legitimate security tooling, discussed in a research or defensive context
- Screenshots and descriptions of malicious content for awareness, without live links or working samples

**Enforcement**

Immediate permanent suspension for distributing malware or phishing links. Security educators who post a live sample by accident should expect removal and a warning rather than a ban.

- Fake official or "backup" accounts for real people or brands
- Using AI to make a real person appear to say or do something they did not

Allowed:

**Enforcement**

- First offense: content removal, profile reset, or a warning
- Repeat offense: escalating suspensions
- Severe offense: impersonating Clannect staff, or impersonating anyone in order to scam or harass, results in immediate permanent suspension

## 13. Privacy and personal information

You may not publish someone else's private or identifying information without their permission.

Not allowed:

- Home addresses, private phone numbers, private email addresses
- Government IDs, financial details, and private documents
- Private account details, private messages, and private communications
- Real names, workplaces, or schools published in order to expose or target someone
- Asking others to find or publish this information

Allowed:

- Information the person has clearly published about themselves
- Business contact details published for that purpose
- Discussion of public figures acting in public roles

**Faces and video**

We do not ban every face in a video. What matters is whether the person had a reasonable expectation of privacy and whether they are being targeted.

- A stranger walking through the background of a public clip: allowed
- Filming someone specifically to embarrass, expose, or mock them: not allowed
- Identifying a private person and directing attention at them: not allowed

**Enforcement**

- First offense: content removal and a warning
- Repeat offense: 7-day, then 30-day suspension
- Severe offense: publishing information to enable harassment or physical harm results in immediate permanent suspension

## 14. Copyright and other people's work

Only upload content you have the right to upload. That means content you made, content you licensed, content you have permission to use, or content you are using in a way the law allows.

Some uses are normal on a gaming platform and generally fine:

- Gameplay clips and recordings
- Commentary, reviews, reactions, and analysis
- Memes, edits, and transformative work
- Fan art and fan content

Some uses are usually a problem:

- Full or substantial reuploads of films, TV, or other people's videos
- Reuploading another creator's clip as your own
- Distributing paid content, game files, or software without authorization
- Full music tracks used as the substance of a post rather than as background

We are not a court and we do not make final legal determinations. We respond to valid copyright complaints, we honor counter-notices, and we remove content when required to.

**Enforcement**

- Valid complaint: content removal and a copyright strike
- Repeat infringement: escalating restrictions and, for accounts that accumulate repeated strikes, permanent suspension
- Severe offense: accounts that exist mainly to distribute infringing material, or that upload pirated software, are removed immediately

Contact support@clannect.com for copyright complaints, counter-notices, and strike appeals.

## 15. AI-generated content

AI content is welcome on Clannect. Deception using AI content is not.

**Label it.** Apply the **AI-Generated** label when realistic AI media could reasonably be mistaken for real footage, photography, audio, or a real person. Obvious art, stylized images, and clearly fictional output do not need a label.

Not allowed:

- Realistic AI media of real people or real events, posted without a label in a way that misleads
- AI used for impersonation, fraud, harassment, or fabricated evidence
- AI-generated sexual content involving real people or minors, which falls under Rules 2 and 6
- Mass-produced AI content posted to farm engagement, which falls under Rule 16

**Enforcement**

- Missing label: label applied, then removal and a warning for repeat cases
- Deceptive use: enforced under the rule being broken, which usually means impersonation, misinformation, or fraud

## 18. Ban and moderation evasion

If your account is suspended or restricted, wait it out. Do not work around it.

Not allowed:

- Creating or using another account while suspended
- Using alternate accounts to get around posting, chat, or content restrictions
- Using another person's account to continue restricted activity
- Asking someone else to post on your behalf while you are suspended

**Enforcement**

The alternate account is permanently suspended and the original suspension is extended or made permanent. Evasion used to continue harassing someone results in permanent suspension of all accounts involved.

## Content labels

Labels let people choose what they see. Apply the right one when you post.

| Label | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Disturbing Content** | Simulated gore, horror, intense fictional violence, and other disturbing imagery |
| **Flashing Lights** | Rapid flashing, strobing, and rapidly alternating imagery. Shown before playback begins. |
| **Strong Language** | Frequent or extreme profanity |
| **Mature Themes** | Mature or heavy themes without graphic imagery |
| **Spoiler** | Significant story or gameplay spoilers |
| **AI-Generated** | Realistic AI media that could be mistaken for real |

How labels work:

- **Disturbing Content**, **Flashing Lights**, and **AI-Generated** are mandatory when they apply. The others are strongly encouraged.
- Labeled content is blurred behind a confirmation screen, and thumbnails are blurred.
- **Flashing Lights** warnings appear before playback starts, not during.
- Labeled content may appear less often in recommendations and may be hidden from users who opt out of it.
- Moderators can apply, change, or remove a label. Removing a label a moderator applied, or mislabeling content to reach a wider audience, is treated as a violation of the underlying rule.

## Enforcement overview

Most violations follow this ladder:

1. **Content removal** and, where relevant, a label applied
2. **Warning**, which is recorded on your account
3. **7-day suspension**
4. **30-day suspension**
5. **Permanent suspension**

We move faster when the behavior is severe, targeted, or repeated after a suspension. We move slower when the mistake was clearly accidental and you correct it.

How we classify behavior:

| Classification | What it means | Typical response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Accidental** | An understandable mistake, no intent to harm | Removal, explanation, sometimes a warning |
| **Reckless** | You did not take reasonable care despite foreseeable consequences | Warning or suspension, depending on severity |
| **Repeated reckless** | The behavior continues after a warning and an explanation | Escalating suspensions |
| **Deliberate** | You knowingly continue prohibited behavior or deliberately deceive | Long suspension or permanent suspension |
| **Malicious** | You are trying to cause serious harm or exploit someone | Immediate permanent suspension, and referral to authorities where appropriate |

Warnings and suspensions stay on your record. Older violations carry less weight over time, and a clean year of activity effectively resets minor violations. Severe violations do not expire.

## Severe violations

These may result in immediate permanent suspension on a first offense, regardless of your history:

- Child sexual abuse material or the sexualization of minors
- Deliberate attempts to trigger someone's medical condition, including targeted flashing content
- Malware and phishing distribution
- Credential and account theft
- Serious fraud, including fake giveaways designed to steal money or information
- Non-consensual intimate imagery
- Credible threats of violence
- Deliberate, malicious, targeted harassment
- Terrorism, trafficking, and criminal exploitation
- False information spread deliberately to cause serious real-world harm

Where the law requires or permits it, we preserve evidence and refer these cases to the appropriate authorities.

## Appeals

You can appeal content removals, warnings, suspensions, and permanent bans.

1. Open the moderation notice you received and select **Appeal**, or email support@clannect.com with your username and the date of the action.
2. Explain why you believe the decision was wrong. New context and evidence help far more than restating that you disagree.
3. We review and respond. Most appeals are answered within 7 days.

When we review an appeal we check whether the right rule was applied, whether the evidence supports the decision, whether the penalty was proportionate, and whether anything was missed. Permanent bans get a second reviewer who was not involved in the original decision.

You get one appeal per action, plus a further review if you can provide genuinely new evidence. Appeals submitted in bulk or used to harass the moderation team may be closed without review.

## Reporting content

Use the report button on any post, comment, message, or profile. Tell us which rule you think was broken and include relevant context.

Reports are confidential. Do not post public callouts asking people to report or pile onto someone, which is harassment under Rule 1. Repeatedly filing false reports to punish someone is also a violation.

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, or you want to understand a moderation decision, contact us at support@clannect.com.
