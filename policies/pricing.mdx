# Clannect Pricing

Last Updated: 3/2/2026

This Pricing page outlines the current status of paid features on Clannect and provides information about planned premium offerings.

## 1. Current Pricing Status

At this time, Clannect does not sell any products, subscriptions, or digital currency.

- No paid memberships are active.
- No in app currency is available for purchase.
- No user payments are currently processed.

All core features that are publicly available can be used without payment.

## 2. Planned Premium Features

Clannect is preparing to introduce premium features and digital purchases in the near future. The following pricing models are currently under consideration.

### 2.1 Clannect Elite Subscription

We are planning to launch:

**Clannect Elite Subscription**  
Estimated Price: 10 USD per month

Potential benefits may include:

- Exclusive platform features  
- Enhanced profile customization  
- Early access to new tools  
- Premium badges or status indicators  
- Additional gamification advantages  

Final features and pricing may change before launch.

### 2.2 Cloin Digital Currency

We are also planning to introduce a platform based digital currency called **Cloin**.

Example pricing under consideration:

- 100 Cloin = 5 USD  
- 250 Cloin = 10 USD  

Cloin may be used for digital enhancements, platform utilities, and other in app features once activated.

All currency pricing, bundles, and use cases are subject to change prior to official release.

## 3. Future Billing and Payments

When paid features become available:

- Payments may be processed through secure third party providers.
- Subscription plans may renew automatically unless canceled.
- Detailed billing terms will be published before launch.

Users will have access to clear pricing information before making any purchase.

## 4. Changes to Pricing

Clannect reserves the right to modify pricing, subscription structures, currency bundles, and feature access at any time.

Any changes will be reflected on this page and, where required, communicated in advance.

## 5. Transparency Commitment

We are committed to providing transparent and clear pricing before activating any paid services. No user will be charged without explicit action and confirmation.
