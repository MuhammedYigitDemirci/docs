# Clannect Pricing

**Last Updated: 8/21/2026**

This Pricing page outlines the current paid features and in-app currency available on Clannect, providing transparent information about our premium offerings.

## 1. Current Pricing Status

Clannect currently offers two types of paid products:

- **Clannect Premium**, a monthly premium subscription priced at **\$4.99 USD per month**.
- **Cubes**, Clannect's in-app currency, available in multiple purchase bundles.

Payments are processed securely through our third-party payment provider.

All core features that are publicly available can be used without payment.

## 2. Clannect Premium Subscription

**Clannect Premium: \$4.99 USD per month**

Clannect Premium is our premium subscription, offering additional benefits for dedicated members of the community.

Premium benefits include:

- Exclusive platform cosmetics and an animated profile frame
- Custom username color visible across feeds and comments
- Premium-only cosmetic drops not available in the Store
- Higher upload quality and advanced post editor
- Detailed profile analytics, including reach and engagement
- Early access to new features and seasonal content
- **500 Cubes every month**
- **10% discount on eligible Store items**
- **300 bonus Cubes on every Cubes purchase**

### 2.1 Cancellation

Users may cancel their Clannect Premium subscription at any time. Upon cancellation, Premium benefits will remain active until the end of the current billing period. No further subscription charges will be made after cancellation.

### 2.2 Refund Policy

All Clannect Premium purchases are final. Once a subscription is activated and Premium benefits are made available to the user, refunds cannot be issued. By completing your purchase, you acknowledge and agree to this policy.

We encourage users to review the features and benefits of Clannect Premium before subscribing.

## 3. Cubes

**Cubes** are Clannect's in-app currency and can be used for eligible cosmetic purchases, customization options, and other in-app enhancements available through the Clannect Store.

Cubes are available in the following bundles:

| Cubes | Price |
| --: | --: |
| **110 Cubes** | **\$1.00 USD** |
| **605 Cubes** | **\$4.99 USD** |
| **1,320 Cubes** | **\$9.99 USD** |
| **2,750 Cubes** | **\$19.99 USD** |
| **7,700 Cubes** | **\$49.99 USD** |
| **16,500 Cubes** | **\$99.99 USD** |

Cubes are a virtual, in-app currency and have no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for real-world currency or transferred outside of Clannect.

Cubes purchased through Clannect are intended for use within the Clannect platform and may be subject to the availability and terms of individual Store items.

## 4. Changes to Pricing

Clannect reserves the right to modify pricing, subscription structures, Cubes bundles, and feature access at any time.

Any changes will be reflected on this page and, where required, communicated to users in advance.

## 5. Transparency Commitment

We are committed to providing clear and honest pricing information at all times.

No user will be charged without explicit action and confirmation.
