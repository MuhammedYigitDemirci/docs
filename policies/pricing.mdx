# Clannect Pricing

Last Updated: 3/19/2026

This Pricing page outlines the current status of paid features on Clannect and provides transparent information about our premium offerings.

## 1. Current Pricing Status

Clannect currently offers one active paid product: the **Clannect Elite** subscription.

- Clannect Elite is available for purchase at **9.99 USD per month**.
- Payments are processed securely through our third party payment provider.
- Subscriptions renew automatically each month unless canceled by the user.

All core features that are publicly available can be used without payment.

## 2. Clannect Elite Subscription

**Clannect Elite**
Price: 9.99 USD per month

Clannect Elite is our premium subscription, offering an enhanced experience for dedicated members of the community. Benefits include:

- Exclusive platform cosmetics and animated profile frame
- Custom username color visible across feeds and comments
- Elite-only cosmetic drops not available in the store
- Higher upload quality and advanced post editor
- Detailed profile analytics including reach and engagement
- Early access to new features and seasonal content

### 2.1 Cancellation

Users may cancel their Clannect Elite subscription at any time. Upon cancellation, Elite benefits will remain active until the end of the current billing period. No further charges will be made after cancellation.

### 2.2 Refund Policy

All Clannect Elite purchases are final. Once a subscription is activated and Elite benefits are made available to the user, refunds cannot be issued. By completing your purchase, you acknowledge and agree to this policy.

We encourage users to review the features and benefits of Clannect Elite before subscribing.

## 3. Planned Features

Clannect is also preparing to introduce **Cloin**, a platform based digital currency for cosmetic purchases and in app enhancements. Pricing and availability will be announced ahead of launch.

All Cloin pricing, bundles, and use cases are subject to change prior to official release.

## 4. Changes to Pricing

Clannect reserves the right to modify pricing, subscription structures, and feature access at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page and, where required, communicated to users in advance.

## 5. Transparency Commitment

We are committed to providing clear and honest pricing information at all times. No user will be charged without explicit action and confirmation.
