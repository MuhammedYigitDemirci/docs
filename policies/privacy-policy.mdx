# Clannect Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 3/2/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clannect collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you access or use our platform, including our website, applications, and related services.

By creating an account or using Clannect, you acknowledge that you have read and accepted this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, you must not use the platform.

## 1. Acceptance of This Policy

By registering for an account on Clannect, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Your continued use of the platform after any updates to this policy constitutes acceptance of the revised version.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate and improve Clannect.

### 2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

- Email address  
- Username  
- Account status information  
- Basic authentication and security data  

This information is required for account creation, login, security monitoring, and core platform functionality.

### 2.2 Usage Information

We may collect limited technical and usage information such as:

- Log data  
- Device and browser information  
- Interaction data within the platform  
- Error and diagnostic information  

This data helps us maintain system stability, prevent abuse, and improve performance.

### 2.3 Communications

If you contact us directly, we may retain the information you provide in order to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly for legitimate platform operations, including:

- Creating and managing user accounts  
- Providing access to Clannect features  
- Maintaining security and preventing abuse  
- Communicating with users about important updates  
- Improving platform performance and user experience  
- Complying with legal obligations  

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Data Sharing

Clannect does not voluntarily sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited data only in the following cases:

- With trusted service providers who help us operate the platform  
- If required by law, regulation, or legal process  
- To protect the rights, safety, and integrity of Clannect, its users, or the public  

Any third party services integrated into Clannect are governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the practices of third party providers.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to:

- Provide services  
- Comply with legal obligations  
- Resolve disputes  
- Enforce our agreements  

We may retain certain data for security or compliance purposes even after account deletion where legally required.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Request access to your personal data  
- Request correction of inaccurate information  
- Request deletion of your personal data  
- Withdraw consent where applicable  

To request data deletion or exercise your rights, please contact us at:

support@clannect.com

We will respond within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legal and operational requirements.

## 7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee 100 percent protection against all potential risks.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Clannect is not intended for individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that such data has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

## 9. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed and stored in jurisdictions different from your country of residence. By using Clannect, you consent to such processing in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time.

Changes become effective once published on the platform. Your continued use of Clannect after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

support@clannect.com

By using Clannect, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
